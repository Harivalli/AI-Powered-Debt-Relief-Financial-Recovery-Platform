1. Problem Statement
Many borrowers struggle to manage multiple loans, understand repayment options, and negotiate settlements with financial institutions. Existing debt management systems provide limited personalized guidance and require significant manual effort. Borrowers often lack financial insights, making it difficult to choose the best recovery strategy.
Goal: Develop an AI-powered debt relief platform that analyzes borrower financial profiles, predicts settlement opportunities, generates personalized negotiation strategies, and assists users in making informed financial recovery decisions using Generative AI.
